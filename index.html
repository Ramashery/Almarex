INTRO Who We Are

Aviation experience. Commercial vision. Global connections.

Almarex is a Dubai-based aviation company delivering commercial, strategic and operational solutions across the global aviation industry. We partner with airlines, airports, governments, investors and logistics companies to unlock growth opportunities, develop new markets and execute complex aviation projects.

Our approach combines decades of airline expertise with regional market intelligence and a global network of industry relationships.

PLR Our Platform

Aviation

Commercial aviation solutions for airlines worldwide: airline representation (GSA/GSSA), route development, network planning, commercial strategy, airline startup and ACMI & charter.

02 — CGO

Cargo

Integrated cargo and logistics development: cargo representation, cargo charter, air freight solutions, cargo network development and supply chain projects.

03 — DEV

Development

Building aviation businesses and infrastructure: airport development, aviation investments, business advisory, public-private partnerships and infrastructure projects.
